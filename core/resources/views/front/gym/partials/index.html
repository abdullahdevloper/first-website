PRESTIGIOUS POSITION IN R&D

FIRST R&D consists of about 100 engineers of which 4 engineers have more than 40 years of experience and 40 engineers have more than 10 years of experience in the friction material area. FIRST R&D has the ability to develop and produce advanced formulas according to the different needs of our customers and the market.

FIRST invented 1:5 scaled dyno test machine with environmental control to conduct core development of formulation, bench tests of different materials, and friction tests for Chinese National standard and CAAM (China Association of Automobile Manufactures) standard of brake pads.

1:5 SCALED DYNO TEST MACHINE

This development method provides us with more effective data compared with full-scale dyno test. Better for the formulation and process optimization enable to carry out in-depth investigation of some difficult problems, such as Creep-Groan noise.
